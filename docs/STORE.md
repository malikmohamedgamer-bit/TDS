## Short description (one line)

A 3D tower defense in your browser: Magic and Modern War against the Blight, solo or with up to 3 friends.

## Long description

The Blight pours out of the rift, and only your keep stands in its way.

Build towers from two rival factions: rifles, rockets and gunships from the Modern War arsenal, or
fire, frost, storms and spirits from the Magic orders. Upgrade them, choose a path, and evolve them
into something much worse for the Blight. Walk the battlefield as your Commander, call in abilities,
and hold the line through 20 waves and their bosses.

- **105 towers** across two factions, including a strike jet and a storm drake, each with upgrade paths
  and evolutions
- **200 enemies and 100 bosses**, from swarming crawlers to bosses that cloak, shield, teleport and
  open portals
- **68 maps on 14 themes**: winding roads, split lanes, roads that cross themselves, and air routes
  where fliers cut straight to your keep
- **10 heroes** with 4 abilities each
- **11 modes**: Campaign, Endless, Boss Rush, a Daily Challenge with modifiers, Time Attack,
  Mutation, War Chest, Last Stand, Skirmish, Challenge and a Sandbox
- **Online co-op for 2-4 players**: share a room code and defend one keep together, each with your
  own hero, towers and cash
- Quests, achievements, an Index of everything you have met, summon banners and cosmetics, all earned
  by playing
- Runs in the browser on desktop and phone, offline after the first visit. No account, no ads, no
  purchases
